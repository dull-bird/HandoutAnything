# Recent research inserts

Use these as short "new research" callouts when the classic material benefits from a modern anchor.

## Memory

| Insert | Why it matters | Suggested placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Augmenting hippocampal–prefrontal neuronal synchrony during sleep enhances memory consolidation in humans** (Nature Neuroscience, 2023) | Gives causal human evidence that precise sleep-phase coordination improves recognition memory. | Memory biology or sleep-related memory sections. |
| **Spindle-locked ripples mediate memory reactivation during human NREM sleep** (Nature Communications, 2024) | Reinforces the idea that memory replay depends on fine-grained sleep oscillation timing. | Sleep, consolidation, or memory-mechanism inserts. |
| **Selection of experience for memory by hippocampal sharp wave ripples** (Science, 2024) | Suggests that replay is selective, not just passive reactivation. | Add after encoding/replay discussion. |

## Perception

| Insert | Why it matters | Suggested placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Network mechanisms of ongoing brain activity’s influence on conscious visual perception** (Nature Communications, 2024) | Shows that prestimulus brain state shifts recognition sensitivity and criterion. | Perception or illusion sections. |
| **A shared code for perceiving and imagining objects in human ventral temporal cortex** (Science, 2026) | Connects perception and imagery, useful for dream and mental imagery discussions. | Perception, imagination, or dream sections. |
| **Human high-order thalamic nuclei gate conscious perception** (Science, 2024) | Useful for a brief note on the thalamus as a perception gate rather than a passive relay. | Perception, awareness, or consciousness inserts. |

## AI and embodied intelligence

| Insert | Why it matters | Suggested placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Catalyzing next-generation Artificial Intelligence through NeuroAI** (Nature Communications, 2023) | Gives the embodied Turing test and a neuroscience-to-AI framing for sensorimotor intelligence. | First lecture bridge, especially if the unit begins with perception. |
| **Extending Minds with Generative AI** (Nature Communications, 2025) | Makes the hybrid-thinking point explicit: humans already extend cognition with external tools. | Unit overview or the first perception-related section. |
| **Embodied large language models enable robots to complete complex tasks in unpredictable environments** (Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025) | Shows the current move from pure text models to embodied agents with action loops. | After perception, memory, or action-related discussion. |
| **Towards shared embodied intelligence in humanoid robots through optimization, development and testing of the human-aware ergoCub robot** (Nature Machine Intelligence, 2026) | A fresh example of shared intelligence plus embodied cognition in collaborative robots. | Optional concluding modern extension. |

## Rule of thumb

- Use only one or two research inserts per chapter.
- Prefer one line of interpretation over a long literature dump.
- If the result is too technical to explain in one paragraph, skip it.
