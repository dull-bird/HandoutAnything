# AI and embodied intelligence

Use these as short bridge notes when the handout needs to connect psychology to current AI.

## Core idea

- Classical perception research treats perception as active construction, not passive reception.
- Modern AI is moving in the same direction: from disembodied pattern recognition toward systems that perceive, remember, predict, and act in a loop.
- For the first lecture, the cleanest bridge is this: `stimulus -> internal model -> action -> new input`.

## How to write the bridge

- One sentence for the classic psychology idea.
- One sentence for the modern AI parallel.
- One sentence explaining the limit: AI can model the loop, but that does not automatically mean human-like experience.

## Good wording

- "这和今天的具身智能很像：系统不是先有一个静态答案，再去看世界，而是在感知、记忆和行动之间不断闭环。"
- "第一讲的重点是知觉的主动建构；现代 AI 则把这种主动建构做成了工程系统。"

## Avoid

- Do not turn this into a long AI survey.
- Do not include more than one bridge paragraph per chapter.
- Do not use this insert in chapters unrelated to perception, memory, consciousness, or action.
